# Factorio GeoGuessr Mod

A GeoGuessr-style minigame for Factorio that challenges players to guess locations based on a limited camera view. Are you waiting for your science to finish? Did you build a mega base and not sure what to do next? Play a round of Geoguessr to see if you remember what you build!
---

## Features

- Random Location Guessing  
  Display a random location on a surface and challenges the player to guess the exact location.

- Customizable Round Timer  
  Choose from 5 seconds up to 10 minutes per round.

- Multiple Rounds  
  Play 1, 3, 5, 10, or 15 rounds per game.

- Surface Selection  
  Use the current surface or include all available surfaces.

- Zoom Control  
  Adjustable location view zoom levels for difficulty tuning.

- Entity-Based Filtering  
  Require a minimum number of entities in a location to avoid empty areas. Unless you are confident you can guess the location without player entites, then by all means, set this to 0

- Blacklist System  
  Exclude specific surfaces or entity types.

- Distance Scoring  
  Score is based on how close the guess is and how much time is left on the clock.

- Disable Identifiers 
  Option to disable recipe icons, tags, train stations, and other helpful identifiers (Warning: if you turn this on, you will have to manually turn them back on after the game)

---

## How to Play

1. Open the GeoGuessr menu using the shortcut or the hotkey (Default Ctrl+G)
2. Configure your game settings
3. Click Start  
4. Observe the location through the camera view  
5. Click on where you think the location is (You can guess in both remote view and in player view)
6. Confirm your guess  
7. Receive your score and distance  
8. Continue to the next round
